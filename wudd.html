Wudd · In development

Low light, warm wood, a lavta, breath. Listening together, before birth.

A dedicated sound and breath practice for pregnancy, under research and construction in Glasgow by Ioannis Valasakis. Small test sessions by invitation, autumn 2026 to winter 2027.

Notice: Wudd is an artistic and sensory listening practice exploring somatic sound resonance. It is not a clinical service, healthcare intervention, or medical therapy.
